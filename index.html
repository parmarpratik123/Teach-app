Approvers

Automatic email notifications

When someone submits work, the next approver gets an email by itself: Manager first, then CEO, and the creator is emailed when changes are requested or the work is approved. The email has a link that opens the item for them.

How to set up automatic sending (5 minutes, free)
  1. Create a free account at emailjs.com and open Email Services → Add new service (Gmail or Outlook). Copy the Service ID.
  2. Open Email Templates → Create new template. Set To email to {{to_email}}, Subject to {{subject}}, Content to {{message}}, and Reply-to to {{reply_to}}. Save and copy the Template ID.
  3. Open Account → General and copy the Public Key.
  4. Paste the 3 codes above, press Save, then Send me a test email.

These codes are saved in this browser only. Enter them once on each device that submits or approves. Open the site from a web address (not from a file on your computer) so the email link works for everyone.
